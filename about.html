About Us

Built on Precision. Backed by Experience.

Founded in 2016 on the belief that every project deserves precision, professionalism, and a partner who delivers.

Founded in 2016, One Point Construction was built on a simple belief: every project deserves precision, professionalism, and a team that delivers on its promises.

With over 25 years of hands-on experience in the construction industry, our leadership has guided projects of all sizes — from roofing and siding to complete interior finishes — with the same level of care and craftsmanship.

We've proudly partnered on multi-family and commercial projects across Indiana, Ohio, Oklahoma, Georgia, and Florida. No matter the location, our commitment stays the same.
