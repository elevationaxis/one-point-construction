One Point is built on relationships — with clients, partners, and the people who show up every day to get it done.

Leadership Spotlight

David Trackwell

Owner / President

David founded One Point Construction after years of learning the business from the ground up — estimating, project management, and operations — and built it into a company capable of taking on projects hundreds of miles from home. He's still hands-on today: bidding jobs, managing relationships with general contractors, and staying close to what's happening on every project.

For David, relationships come before the next job — and that shows most when a project doesn't go according to plan.

DrivenCompetitiveLoyal

“Construction doesn't always go perfectly. How you communicate, respond, and take care of people when something goes wrong says more about a company than when everything goes right.”

David Trackwell, Owner / President

Outside of work, David's coached travel softball with the Beverly Bandits for nearly a decade, helping dozens of young athletes earn Division I scholarships along the way. Family comes first — from watching daughter Addisyn begin her own D1 softball career at Louisville, to being a first-time grandpa.

“Have faith, keep moving forward, and control the things you can control.”David's advice for staying the course

Carl Henry

Project Manager

Carl's been in construction since he was 13, learning the trade alongside his dad — painting, staining, and prepping — before working his way up to running full-scale jobsites. Today he manages nearly every moving piece of a project: crew scheduling, materials, subcontractors, warranty service, and keeping GCs and customers in the loop from start to finish.

“I take pride in my work and go above and beyond to make sure the customer is satisfied.”

HonestCaringRespectful

When he's not on a jobsite, Carl's usually building something of his own — RC cars, minibikes, engines — or manning the grill for his fiancée, two daughters, and three dogs. Buying his first home is still one of the moments he's most proud of.

“Don't bite the hand that feeds you.”Carl's best advice

Field Operations

Michael B. Ferguson

Senior Project Manager

Michael has been around construction nearly his entire life, first learning the trade at just six years old from his stepfather, a Master Carpenter. That early exposure grew into a lifelong career in the industry, along with formal trade apprenticeship training and years of hands-on field experience.

At One Point, Michael helps keep jobsites running smoothly and projects moving forward. He oversees crews and day-to-day field operations and specializes in cabinet installation and railings. One of his proudest professional accomplishments has been independently managing and completing multiple projects in Florida.

LoyalHardworkingRelentless

“The best part about being part of One Point is having the opportunity to help build this company from the ground up alongside my brother and best friend.”

Michael B. Ferguson, Senior Project Manager

Outside of work, Michael is a devoted father of three — Kayden, Jordan, and Izzy — and has been with his significant other for 18 years. He enjoys fishing, hunting, looking for shark teeth, spending time at the beach, and reading. He's also a Colts, Pacers, and Cubs fan, and Marco Island is one of his favorite places to get away.

“Always put God first.”Michael's advice for staying grounded

Brand & Digital Strategy

Brittany Moulder

Digital Strategy & Marketing Partner, Elevation Axis LLC

Brittany leads digital strategy, branding, and marketing for One Point through Elevation Axis LLC. She works closely with David to turn the company's vision into a clear, cohesive brand — from the website and content to the systems and strategy that support continued growth. Her role is making sure One Point's digital presence reflects the same quality, experience, and attention to detail the team brings to every project.

For Brittany, the work is especially personal. David and Michael were two of her very first friends and were like older brothers to her growing up. Being able to use the company she built and the skills she developed to help grow something they've built makes her role with One Point feel incredibly full-circle.

CreativeLoyalRelentless

“I've always been drawn to seeing what something could become — and figuring out how to help get it there.”

Brittany Moulder, Digital Strategy & Marketing Partner

Outside of work, Brittany is a mom of three and enjoys traveling, concerts, sporting events, reading, and spending time near the ocean. She never planned on becoming a business owner, but following her curiosity across strategy, creativity, technology, and problem-solving eventually became Elevation Axis.

“You don't have to have the whole thing figured out to take the next step.”Brittany's advice for moving forward
